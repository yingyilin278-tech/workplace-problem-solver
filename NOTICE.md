# Notice

## Scope

`workplace-problem-solver` is an AI-agent workflow for organizing workplace and job-search questions. It produces decision support, draft wording, action options and risk reminders. It does not make decisions for the user.

## Third-party sources

The repository references books, academic papers, talks, platforms and other public materials. Names, quotations, trademarks and source materials remain the property of their respective owners.

Knowledge cards are intended to record source metadata and transform limited ideas into original workflow elements such as decision rules, questions, counterexamples and safety boundaries. They are not substitutes for the original works. If a card contains inaccurate attribution or more source expression than necessary, please open an Issue with the affected file and source.

## Privacy

The repository does not require an account, telemetry service or hosted database. Local scripts should not transmit user content unless a user separately connects them to an external service.

Do not commit:

- real names, phone numbers, addresses, identity numbers or private contact details;
- resumes, contracts, performance records or chat logs without authorization;
- company secrets, internal documents or customer data;
- API keys, cookies, tokens or credentials;
- copyrighted full text that you do not have permission to distribute.

Users remain responsible for the privacy and data-processing behavior of the Agent, model provider, plugins and runtime they choose.

## Professional boundaries

This project is not a law firm, medical provider, emergency service or mental-health service. It must not be treated as a source of definitive legal or medical conclusions. High-stakes outputs require human review and, where appropriate, qualified local professional support.
