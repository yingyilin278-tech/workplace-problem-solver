## Required user information

- The current team size, stage, and stability.
- The team's three biggest coordination problems.
- Whether the leader is currently focused on safety, vulnerability, or purpose.
- The cues (words, actions, design) the team reads as belonging signals.

## Applicable roles, power, and culture

Applicable roles:

- Team leaders and managers.
- Coaches working with intact teams.
- HR partners supporting team interventions.

Power asymmetry:

Leader behavior sets the tone. Use the model by changing what the leader does, not by demanding vulnerability from lower-power team members.

Jurisdiction and cultural transfer:

The framework is broadly applicable, but local norms on disclosure, hierarchy, and feedback must be respected.

## Core claims

- Group cultures are built through small, repeated interactions.
- Three pillars: establish safety, share vulnerability, establish purpose.
- Belonging cues—proximity, eye contact, turn-taking, attention—shape team dynamics more than mission statements.
- High-performance teams combine strong purpose with interpersonal safety.

## Decision rules

- If the team is stuck in status competition, raise the cost of zero-sum behaviors and the reward of helping.
- If the leader is not modeling fallibility, the team will not share its mistakes.
- If the team's purpose is vague, ask each member to name the group's purpose in one sentence.
- If the team has high turnover, fix the belonging cues first.

## Action steps

1. Identify the team's top three belonging cues and where they are weakest.
2. Run a team meeting where the leader shares a recent failure and a learning.
3. Ask each team member to describe the team's purpose in one sentence.
4. Track small acts of help and acknowledge them.
5. Review after four weeks.

## Ready-to-use wording

- Audience: team; channel: meeting; timing: weekly. Wording: "本周我看到 [同事] 帮了 [另一同事] 解决了 [问题]，这种帮助是这个团队的核心。"
- Audience: leader; channel: private note; timing: weekly. Wording: "本周我做了哪三件事让团队感到安全？我做的哪三件事可能让他们感到不安？"

## Counterexamples

- Do not confuse vulnerability with oversharing personal trauma.
- Do not use "purpose" rhetoric to mask low pay or unsafe conditions.
- Do not assume a mission statement alone changes culture.

## Contraindications

- Workplaces that require confidentiality about performance or personnel issues.
- High-stakes, regulated work where a less consensual style is required.

## Conflicts and limitations

- Author bias and conflicts: Author is a journalist; framework synthesizes team research.
- Rule conflicts: ['Belonging cues can be misused to enforce conformity.', 'Some safety-critical work requires directive leadership.']
- Copyright scope: Summarize the three pillars. Do not reproduce extended passages or figures.
