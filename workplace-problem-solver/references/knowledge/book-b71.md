## Applicable roles, power, and culture

Applicable roles:

- HR student or practitioner in mainland China
- Manager handling employee relations
- Employer compliance professional
- Employee using the book only to understand process and possible employer reasoning
- Labor-relations educator or adviser

Power asymmetry:

The book is designed partly for enterprise managers and HR practitioners, who control records, procedures, ratings, and proposed employment action. Employee-facing use must add worker-rights sources, preserve a safe response opportunity, and avoid presenting employer compliance strategy as a neutral conclusion.

Jurisdiction and cultural transfer:

The book focuses on mainland China employee-relations compliance and core labor-contract law as of its 2025 publication context. National rules, judicial interpretations, and local arbitration and court practice can change and differ by location. It is not transferable to Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan, or other countries without separate legal research.

## Decision rules

- If an employee-relations issue may affect pay, duties, discipline, contract status, resignation, or termination, then identify current legal authority and local procedure before applying a textbook rule.
- If a user lacks legal background, then use the book to create an issue checklist and vocabulary, not to skip authoritative verification or qualified advice.
- If HR proposes an adverse action, then test the communicated rule, factual evidence, consistency, employee response opportunity, and required procedure before implementation.
- If the user's role changes the likely interpretation, then present employee, manager, HR, and legal-risk perspectives separately.
- If the book's statement conflicts with a later statute, judicial interpretation, authoritative local rule, or qualified local advice, then the current authoritative source takes priority.

## Action steps

1. Identify the user's role, mainland China location, employer type, worker status, and event date.
2. Build a dated chronology and gather relevant documents lawfully.
3. Classify the issue within the employee-relations lifecycle and list the facts that remain disputed.
4. Use the book's chapter structure and practical questions to identify legal and process issues.
5. Verify current statutes, judicial interpretations, local rules, and authoritative guidance.
6. Separate reversible clarification and evidence-preservation steps from signing, resignation, settlement, or termination.
7. Refer high-stakes or deadline-sensitive matters to a qualified local lawyer, union, labor authority, arbitration institution, or court as appropriate.

## Ready-to-use wording

- Employee to HR, written request before responding to an adverse action: "Please provide the current policy and legal basis, the facts and records relied upon, the response deadline, and a copy of every document I am asked to acknowledge or sign."
- HR to manager, internal review before implementation: "Please separate the business concern from the legal ground and provide the communicated standard, dated evidence, comparable treatment, employee response, and required procedure."
- HR to employee, formal and neutral communication: "This meeting concerns the stated issue and proposed process. You may review the materials, provide factual corrections by the deadline, keep a copy, and seek independent advice."

## Counterexamples

- A textbook example may not reflect a later judicial interpretation or the local arbitration and court approach.
- A compliant-looking form does not cure a missing legal ground, false fact, inconsistent rule, or defective procedure.
- A process designed for standard employees may not apply to dispatched workers, platform workers, foreign employees, senior executives, or protected employee groups.
- Employer and employee interests can diverge even when both use the language of harmonious employee relations.
- A signature can indicate receipt, agreement, admission, waiver, or settlement depending on wording and context.

## Contraindications

- Definitive legal advice without current national and local verification

- Substitution of the textbook for qualified legal, union, labor-authority, arbitration, or court support in a high-stakes dispute
