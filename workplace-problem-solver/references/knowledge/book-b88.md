# B88 — An Elegant Puzzle

- Coverage: C8, C12

Official sources:

## Required user information

- The user's current management stage and recent transitions.
- The team's biggest puzzles: alignment, career growth, hiring, performance.
- The user's authority to make structural changes.
- Recent feedback from the team and from peers.

## Applicable roles, power, and culture

Applicable roles:

- Engineering managers and tech leads.
- People managers in technical or product organizations.
- HR partners supporting engineering teams.

Power asymmetry:

The framework emphasizes structural choices; leaders must consider how those choices affect lower-power team members before rolling them out.

Jurisdiction and cultural transfer:

Most puzzles (career, hiring, performance) are universal, but specific compensation, termination, and interview rules are jurisdiction-specific.

## Core claims

- Management puzzles have a finite, learnable shape.
- Career growth is a central responsibility of the manager, not a side task.
- Hiring is the highest-leverage activity; an off-process hire is more expensive than a delayed start.
- Most attrition is preventable when managers act on early signals.

## Decision rules

- If a high performer is at risk of leaving, the manager should meet within the week and explore the cause.
- If a hire is going poorly, slow down and assess against the bar before extending.
- If a team member is not growing, name the gap and design a project that targets it.
- If the manager cannot invest the time required by the framework, redesign the role before quitting the practice.

## Action steps

1. Map each direct report's growth edge and current project alignment.
2. Schedule growth conversations at least quarterly.
3. Audit recent hires against the bar; document decisions and outcomes.
4. Track attrition risk signals monthly.
5. Share the framework with peers to align expectations.

## Ready-to-use wording

- Audience: direct report; channel: one-on-one; timing: quarterly. Wording: "过去一个季度你做得最满意的和最想换的是哪件事？接下来一年你最想发展的能力是什么？"
- Audience: peer manager; channel: written note; timing: when discussing a candidate. Wording: "我对这位候选人的疑虑是 [具体点]，你怎么看？我们要达到什么标准才算通过？"

## Counterexamples

- Do not treat the framework as a substitute for difficult termination decisions.
- Do not use growth conversations to avoid giving real feedback.
- Do not promise promotion without a clear bar.

## Contraindications

- Situations requiring formal PIP or HR-led process.
- Cases where growth is structurally blocked (e.g., reorganization, hiring freeze).

## Conflicts and limitations
