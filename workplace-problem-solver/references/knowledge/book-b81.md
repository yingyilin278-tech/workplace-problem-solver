## Required user information

- Whose voice is being excluded or underweighted in the current decision.
- The decision's reversibility and who bears the cost of exclusion.
- The user's authority to change the design of the meeting, process, or system.
- Any legal or compliance obligations related to the affected group.

## Applicable roles, power, and culture

Applicable roles:

- Managers and team leads designing decision processes.
- DEI partners and program owners.
- Product or service designers building for diverse users.

Power asymmetry:

Inclusion work can become symbolic if it does not change who has decision authority. Distinguish between being heard and having power.

Jurisdiction and cultural transfer:

Local anti-discrimination law, accessibility requirements, and data protection rules take priority over aspirational inclusion language.

## Core claims

- Inclusion is a design choice, not a personality trait.
- Seven principles: acknowledgment, accountability, access, accommodation, attitude, aspiration, action.
- Excluding people from design conversations often leads to products, policies, and processes that fail the same people.
- Listening to dissent improves decision quality and reduces downstream harm.

## Decision rules

- If a decision affects a group with less power, design the process to actively invite and weight their input.
- If dissent is missing, ask whether the meeting format is suppressing it.
- If the user's team has low psychological safety, fix the format and the manager before adding training.
- If an inclusion initiative has no measurable change in decision outcomes, redesign it.

## Action steps

1. Map the stakeholders affected by the decision.
2. Identify who has not been at the table historically and why.
3. Redesign the meeting or process to make participation easier for those stakeholders.
4. Document how their input changed the decision.
5. Review the outcome and adjust the process.

## Ready-to-use wording

- Audience: team; channel: meeting opening; timing: at the start of a project. Wording: "这次决策会影响三类用户，我们先确认他们是否有机会参与，并预留时间听取他们的反馈。"
- Audience: manager; channel: written note; timing: when reviewing outcomes. Wording: "我们这次决策有 A、B、C 三个分歧点，请说明每个分歧中谁的观点被采纳、谁没有。"

## Counterexamples

- Do not use inclusion language to delay a difficult decision indefinitely.
- Do not assume one affected person can represent an entire group.
- Do not treat inclusion as a substitute for legal compliance.

## Contraindications

- Decisions subject to formal regulatory procedure.
- Privacy or confidentiality constraints that prevent certain voices from participating.
- Active grievance or investigation processes that require separate handling.
